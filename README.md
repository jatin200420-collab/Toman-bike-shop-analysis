# Toman-bike-shop-analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing Toman Bike Share's revenue, profitability, rider behavior, and pricing opportunities using **SQL and Power BI**.

### 🔍 Project Overview
The project combines two years of bike-sharing data (2021–2022) with cost information to understand business performance and identify opportunities for improving revenue and profitability.

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- SQL – Data cleaning, transformation, joining and analysis
- Power BI – Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- DAX – Calculated measures and KPIs
- CSV – Source datasets

### 📊 Key Analysis
- Analyzed hourly revenue and profit trends
- Compared revenue and profitability across 2021 and 2022
- Studied seasonal revenue performance
- Analyzed registered vs. casual riders
- Identified peak hours and high-demand periods
- Evaluated the impact of pricing on revenue and profitability

### 💡 Key Insights
- Revenue increased significantly from 2021 to 2022 despite an increase in ride price.
- Summer generated the strongest seasonal revenue performance.
- Peak profitability was observed during approximately 10 AM–6 PM.
- Registered riders accounted for around 81% of total riders, highlighting the importance of customer retention and loyalty strategies.
- Analysis indicated potential for further price optimization and dynamic pricing during high-demand periods.

### 🎯 Business Recommendation
Based on the analysis, a **10–15% price increase** was identified as a potentially viable strategy, with a suggested price range of approximately **$5.49–$5.74 per ride**. Peak-hour and seasonal demand could also be leveraged through dynamic pricing.

### 📈 Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of:
- Total Riders
- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Revenue & Profit Trends
- Hourly Profitability
- Seasonal Performance
- Rider Demographics

This project demonstrates how **SQL-based data transformation and Power BI visualization can be combined to turn raw business data into actionable insights and pricing recommendations.**
